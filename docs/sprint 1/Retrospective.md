# Sprint 1 Retrospective

**Sprint:** Sprint 1  
**Duration:** 11 August 2025 – 30 August 2025  

---

## 1. What Went Well
- Requirement Elicitation document completed with multiple iterations.  
- Roles and responsibilities were clear and collaboration tools worked effectively.  
- HiFi prototype in Figma was successfully delivered and provided strong clarity for the client.  
- Team demonstrated adaptability by updating requirements after client feedback.  

---

## 2. What Didn’t Go Well
- Project backlog management was partially duplicated across different tools.  
- Story point estimation was not introduced early enough.  
- Diagrams were not as detailed as they could have been.  
- Communication with the client could have been more frequent and higher quality.  
- Artefacts were not always delivered in a timely manner.  

---

## 3. What Can Be Improved
- Integrate **Jira backlog functionality** with the project backlog to avoid redundancy.  
- Introduce **story point estimation earlier** in the sprint to improve planning accuracy.  
- Produce more comprehensive diagrams to support documentation.  
- Increase frequency and quality of communication with the client.  
- Ensure artefacts are updated and shared more promptly.  

---

## 4. Action Items
- [ ] Define a clear process for syncing Jira backlog and project backlog.  
- [ ] Establish story point estimation guidelines and apply them from the start of Sprint 2.  
- [ ] Improve documentation diagrams with greater detail.  
- [ ] Schedule regular client check-ins and improve communication quality.  
- [ ] Set clearer internal deadlines for artefacts to ensure timeliness.  

---

## 5. Out of Scope for Next Sprint
- Burn down chart in Jira will not be used, as it does not provide additional value for this project context.  

---

## 6. Notes
This retrospective reflects feedback and observations from the Sprint 1 review and retro meeting.
