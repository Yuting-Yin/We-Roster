## 2. How the Project Is Going
- **Client Interaction:** Three meetings with the client clarified misunderstandings in early persona development. This led to simplification and correction of the initial overcomplicated scope.  
- **Documentation:** Requirement documents underwent significant updates, reflecting more accurate understanding of client needs.  
- **HiFi Prototype:** Provided clear demonstration value, reducing the necessity of a full-stack demo at this stage.  
- **Overall Status:** Planning goals largely achieved; however, several requirements remain undefined and will require further clarification in upcoming sprints.  

---

## 3. Key Learnings
- HiFi prototypes can sometimes replace early-stage demos if they provide sufficient clarity.  
- Early assumptions about user personas can lead to unnecessary complexity; direct client validation is crucial.  
- Requirement gathering should anticipate ambiguity and plan for progressive clarification.  

---

## 4. Next Steps
- Clarify remaining undefined requirements with the client.  
- Improve level of detail in diagrams and supporting artefacts.  
- Apply story point estimation consistently from Sprint 2 onwards.  
- Continue refining backlog processes in Jira.
